In the light

A nod, not a shout

Your body was keeping your design, not breaking it. Gene Key 38, your Radiance, says you need to move and push your limits or you dry up. The workouts and the walks with Pixie were you doing what you're built for.

The timing. It didn't go mid-workout. It went at the door to stillness, on the way in to prayers. That's someone holding the door open and saying "this way, slower."

The spot. The front of the solar plexus is will, the doing. The back of it is what holds up the one doing the doing. Your body is pointing at the support, not the engine. The engine's fine.

You heard it at the right volume. It didn't have to shout.

In the shadow

Struggle dressed as Perseverance

38's shadow is Struggle, and its gift is Perseverance. The line between them runs straight through recovery. You went to full output while your body was still clearing strep after the antibiotics. Feeling great and being recovered are different things. Your immune system was still on shift, and you gave it a second job.

Open Solar Plexus. You take on the emotional weather of whoever's in the house, and the house is fuller this week. A hypothesis only: some of what's sitting behind your solar plexus may not be yours. "Whose is this?" is your own question.

The hero who stops the train with his own body doesn't ask for help until he's lying unmasked in the subway car.

The deeper read

The one place you can't reach

The mid back is the one place on your body you can't reach yourself. You can't rub it, you can't fully stretch it, and you can't even see it. It needs someone else's hands. You give in the first person and receive in the third, and your body picked the one spot only somebody else can get to. So yes, slow down. But read it less as "do less" and more as "let yourself be held up." The back is support, and Pixie is Level 3 in your own regulation map for a reason.

Who's putting their hands on that spot this week, and have you asked them yet?
